# The 1541's and 1540's DOS: conversion log

The DOS of the 1541 disk drive and the 1540 before it, as one SDK source (plan phase V5 of [the Commodore
plan](../backlog/Commodore.md)): 16 KB at `$C000-$FFFF`, the chips 325302-01 (`$C000`, shared by every revision and
the 1540) and 901229 (`$E000`, the 1541's revisions) or 325303-01 (the 1540's). It is written from the dumps in the
maintained style; Commodore's listing gives the old names only, kept in `Also:` (the plan's answered question 1, the
PET's rule). This log covers steps 1 to 3 of [the method](../ConvertingSources.md), the rename table,
[Dos1541Renames.md](Dos1541Renames.md), and the conversion.

| R1 | 1541, 901229-01 | 325302-01 and 901229-01 |
| R2 | 1541, 901229-02 | 325302-01 and 901229-02 |
| R3 | 1541, 901229-03 | 325302-01 and 901229-03 |
| R5 | 1541, 901229-05 | 325302-01 and 901229-05 |
| R6 | 1541, 901229-06 | 325302-01 and 901229-06 |
| R40 | 1540 | 325302-01 and 325303-01 |

901229-04 has no dump, in MAME or elsewhere this conversion found, so it has no build.

## Decisions

- D1 (2026-11-27). Commodore's listings `DOS_1541_05` and `DOS_1540`, from mist64/cbmsrc at commit `01bd60f`, are
  fetched into the scratchpad for names only, as the plan says; the `_REC` folders, which are third-party
  reconstructions, are not used.
- D2 (2026-11-27). The owner left the rename table's questions to the drafter's judgement after an independent
  review of each part, as K10 did for the kernal's last parts. Each decision is recorded here.
- D3. The DOS's families: `Controller`, `Jobs`, `Commands`, `Directory`, `Bam`, `Files`, `Relative`, `Serial`,
  `Startup`, `Idle` and `Errors`, with the VIAs' registers, the job queue, the shared variables, the ROM's tables
  and its jump vectors top level.
- D4. The table is drafted in six parts: the declarations; the job loop and GCR; the command parser and the
  commands; the directory, the BAM and the files; the relative files; and the serial bus, the idle loop, start-up,
  the errors and the ROM's tables. (The plan's four areas, with the declarations first, as the kernal's table had
  them, and the files split in two for their size.)
- D5. Names cite `D1541`, the 901229-05 listing, only. The drive references the plan fetched (*Inside Commodore
  DOS* and the others) are not in this environment; their names can be added from them later.
- D6. The 1540's listing (`DOS_1540`) is a different build from its 325303-01 chip: assembled, its labels are 201
  or 204 bytes from the 1541's, and 158 of its 8,153 high-chip bytes match the chip. Its names are therefore not
  tied to the chip's addresses; the 1540's places of its own are named from the dumps.
- D7 (2026-11-27). The six parts were each reviewed independently against the code, and every finding was checked
  before it was applied. The reviews corrected names, kinds and reasons (among them the head's direction, which the
  listing's comments give backwards; the controller's seek, which takes the last off-track job, not the nearest; and
  the addresses of the revisions' changes), and settled the names the parts share: the part that defines an address
  owns its name, the others cite it, and no name in a scope repeats a top-level name the scope uses. Each question's
  outcome is below, under step 3.

## Step 1: the inventory

Made by script, outside the repository:

- **The listing.** `DOS_1541_05`: 74 files, 12,194 lines, from `1541.src`. A small assembler for Commodore's syntax
  assembled it, giving every label an address: 1,557 names. Its 15,946 bytes match 325302-01 and 901229-05 at
  every address; the 438 it does not assemble are the chips' fill and patch space. So each name is tied to its
  ROM address directly.
- **The names**, by kind: 1,191 in the ROM (296 routines that are called, 75 more reached only by jumps, 724
  locals, 32 tables and 64 labels nothing reaches), 303 locations (zero page, RAM, the buffers and the two VIAs)
  and 63 constants.
- **A first source.** The dumps disassembled at the listing's instructions, with data where the listing has data
  and the listing's names on the operands that have their values, assemble with the SDK's assembler
  byte-identically to 325302-01 and 901229-05: the base the conversion starts from.
- **The revisions**, against 901229-05 (bytes that differ, and where):
  - 901229-01: 93 bytes in 12 places, in TLKERR, LSNERR, BOOT3, ACP00A, ACP03, DSKINT, SETERR (two), WRT10,
    WRTS20 and ECHKSM, and the patch area, where -01 has neither PATCH5 nor PATCH6 (`$FF10-$FF2E` is fill).
  - 901229-02: 86 bytes, as -01 less ACP03, and with `RSR` in the fill at `$FFE2`; 901229-03: 84 bytes, as -02 less
    WRT10 and WRTS20.
  - 901229-06: 10 bytes, in N101, ECHKSM and a new patch after PATCH6, at `$FF2F-$FF35`.
  - The 1540's 325303-01: 136 bytes, nearest -01 (44 bytes apart), with its own changes in ERRTAB (message 73),
    ISRCLK, MAK10, ECHKSM, the serial speed routines (`$FEF3-$FF0F`, SLOWD, CLKDAT and NNMI, are fill) and the
    processor's NMI vector at `$FFFA`.
  The 325302-01 chip is the same in every build.

## Step 3: the rename table

[Dos1541Renames.md](Dos1541Renames.md), in six parts (D4), settled as follows (D2, D7).

### Part 1: the zero page, the RAM, the VIAs and the constants

- D-P1-Q1. A variable goes in the family whose code uses it and several families' variables are top level; `tst2`-`tst4`
  are `Files`, `tstfnd` is `Bam`, and `trnsfr`'s listing routines are `Directory`, as recommended.
- D-P1-Q2. Each job of a two-job byte gets its own name in its own scope, and one-job synonyms share one definition with
  `Also:`, as recommended.
- D-P1-Q3. VIA registers are named for chip and register with the listing's names in `Also:`, and the job-named aliases
  `DriveControl` and `DiskData` stand, as recommended.
- D-P1-Q4. `JobQueue`, `JobHeaders`, `LastJobs`, `Buffers` and `BufferPositions` stay arrays with strides in their
  comments, as recommended.
- D-P1-Q5. The per-channel and per-file-name tables are top level, named `Channel` or `FileName` plus the field, as
  recommended.
- D-P1-Q6. prefixed constant sets are top level where several families use them and scoped otherwise, as recommended.
- D-P1-Q7. The 23 declarations are dropped and named in prose, and the seven unused VIA registers, CCHKSM and FREEC0
  stay, as recommended.
- D-P1-Q8. The rows say what the code does where the listing's comments disagree, including the 14.8 ms interrupt,
  MAXSA, POTA1, the `$1C08` timer 2 names, PHASE's direction and SYSTS's message, as recommended.

### Part 2: the job loop and GCR

- D-P2-Q1. Everything in jobssf is Jobs and everything in lcc* and irq is Controller; the routines every family calls
  (Jobs::Wait, Read, Write, SetHeader) stay in Jobs, not top level.
- D-P2-Q2. LCC is Controller::ScanJobs with entry Rescan, the job handlers are chained routines, FinishJob and EndPass
  are the two exits, Initialize falls into ScanJobs with an assert, and no single dispatch proc or MainLoop name.
- D-P2-Q3. SYSIRQ is Controller::Interrupt, calling part 6's Serial::AtnInterrupt.
- D-P2-Q4. BGTAB is Controller::GcrCodes (part 1's GTAB is Controller::GcrGroup), GCRHI/GCRLO stay in Controller as
  GcrHighNibbles/GcrLowNibbles, and the Encode/Decode routines are named as listed, with reasons saying bad codes are
  never checked.
- D-P2-Q5. The format is three routines (Format, WriteTrack, VerifyTrack) with an assert at each fall, lccfmt4's pieces
  are routines, FMTERR is Controller::FormatError, and CODE is dropped.
- D-P2-Q6. The tick is VIA 2 timer 1 at about 14.85 ms restarted at the end of each pass, so the reasons give minimum
  times (spin-up 60 ticks, timeout 255, settle 5); the accelerated stepping is kept with a comment that it never runs,
  because STEPS is a signed byte (at most 128) and MINSTP is 200.
- D-P2-Q7. 901229-05's code, with a `.if` on the revision's constant for the header gap (8 bytes in R1, R2 and R40)
  and the 1540's `NOP` at `$FC86`, and the listing's patches described in comments.
- D-P2-Q8. head offsets are half-tracks, positive inward, with locals OffsetHead::StepIn and StepOut.
- D-P2-Q9. HED2TS, TSCHK and VNERR are separate routines in listing order, not one Start proc, and SETLJB is
  Jobs::StartOnLastDrive.
- D-P2-Q10. Read branches to Run, Write falls into it, Run has entries WithDrive and Repeat and falls into Wait, and
  TSTJOB is Jobs::Poll with the recovery as its locals.
- D-P2-Q11. the skipped loads stay inside their routines with the $2C explained, and DONE is Seek's entry JobDone.
- D-P2-Q12. unreferenced labels are dropped with Not referenced, and CNTST is dropped and named in prose.
- D-P2-Q13. ScanJobs fails drive 1 jobs with $0F, the dual-drive leftovers stay with comments, and no name mentions a
  drive number.

### Part 3: the command parser and the commands

- D-P3-Q1. Execute dispatches through part 6's CommandLetters, CommandRoutinesLow/High and CommandPatterns, using part
  1's Commands::VALIDATE, LOAD_STRUCTURE, COUNT and FIRST_PARSED, and each handler is a routine named for its command.
- D-P3-Q2. ENDCMD and CMDERR stay in Commands as Finish (entries KeepBamFlag, Report, NoMessage) and Fail.
- D-P3-Q3. The parser is ScanFor, ParseNames (entries AfterColon, Equals) and Prepare (entry ClearTables); IMAGE's
  bits stay literal masks, each commented.
- D-P3-Q4. The block commands share BlockParameters (entry FromY) and ParseNumber, with scoped aliases for the shared
  parameter bytes, B-P named SetBufferPosition, and BadSyntax an entry of Block.
- D-P3-Q5. M-R, M-W and M-E are one Commands::Memory routine with locals Read, OneByte, Write, BadSyntax, ending without
  Finish, and MEMEX dropped.
- D-P3-Q6. User (entry Reset), UserJump, UserRead and UserWrite in Commands, using part 1's UserVectorsPointer and part
  6's UserCommandVectors.
- D-P3-Q7. The utility loader is Commands::LoadUtility with GetByte and AddToChecksum nested, aliases over Work0-Work4,
  and UTLD20 named ReadCount to avoid the Count alias.
- D-P3-Q8. BOOT is Startup::Boot with its filler as data, and BOOT2-BOOT4 are dropped.
- D-P3-Q9. The directory-listing half of trnsfr goes to Directory, with ToggleDrive and DeleteEntry in Directory and
  FreeChain in Bam.
- D-P3-Q10. OPEN "#" is Files::OpenDirect.
- D-P3-Q11. The copy command is Copy, CopyFiles and CreateCopy with flat helpers, SetUpCopyAll kept, and Duplicate and
  Format in Commands.
- D-P3-Q12. Unreferenced listing-only labels are dropped, two names at one address share one name with Also, and SD50 is
  a label.
- D-P3-Q13. The routines keep two-drive names while the reasons say only the defaults are forced to drive 0.

### Part 4: the directory, the BAM, and the files and channels

- D-P4-Q1. Directory owns everything that reads or writes a directory entry, Files owns OPEN's and CLOSE's own steps,
  and LOADIR is Files::LoadDirectory.
- D-P4-Q2. The channels and buffers are all in Files, with only BMASK (BitMasks) top level.
- D-P4-Q3. The tst2-tst4 helpers are scoped by what they do: INTDRV Commands::Initialize, the disk-reading ones Bam,
  DRTRD/DRTWRT Jobs::ReadTrackSector/WriteTrackSector, NXDRBK Directory::NewBlock, PUTSS Relative::PutSideSectorByte.
- D-P4-Q4. Routines sharing a body are one routine with entries (Directory::FindFile, Directory::Search); a routine that
  only falls into the next stays its own.
- D-P4-Q5. Each address with two or three listing names gets one name with the rest in Also:, and the 19 unreferenced
  labels are dropped.
- D-P4-Q6. WFREE and SCFLG keep names (Bam::FlushAndMarkFree, Files::SetOrClearFlag) with a comment that nothing calls
  them.
- D-P4-Q7. The code's slips (including FRETS's directory-track branch that skips the borrow and falls into the DISK FULL
  check) are documented and not changed; CLRCHN counting to 1 is a difference, not a slip.
- D-P4-Q8. BMASK is top-level BitMasks and MAXSEC top-level SectorsOnTrack, distinct from part 1's
  Controller::SectorCount.
- D-P4-Q9. The two-drive code keeps its structure, named for what it does (Directory::ChooseDrives,
  Directory::SearchOrder), with comments that drive 1 never answers.
- D-P4-Q10. INTDRV is Commands::Initialize and VERDIR is Directory::Validate, with VMKBAM as Directory::MarkChain.
- D-P4-Q11. No local is named for a scope or a top-level name; the colliding locals (CommandWaiting, Channel,
  RecordSize, Track, NoDrive) are renamed.

### Part 5: the relative files and the side sectors

- D-P5-Q1. The channel's buffer routines (GETACT, GAFLGS, CURBLK, GETHDR, RDAB, WRTOUT, SCRUB and the rest) are Files'
  scope, with CDIRTY as Files::MarkClean; GAFLGS is called only by Files and Relative code.
- D-P5-Q2. The relative-file-only helpers (SETLNK, GETLNK, NULLNK, SET00, B0TOB0, CLRBUF, NULBUF, ADDNR, SDIRTY) are
  Relative's; part 4's PUTSS is Relative::PutSideSectorByte.
- D-P5-Q3. Tsutil's job-starter tail is Files::StartBlockJob with ClearDirty and Start entries; RDSS/WRTSS are
  Relative's short routines, RDSS's bpl is taken only for an allocated side-sector buffer and otherwise falls into SJ10.
- D-P5-Q4. RDIN and IBWT are unreached code kept with a comment, and the labels that mark only fallen-into code (GA2,
  RELP06, WR20, WR51, RD10, NS20) are dropped.
- D-P5-Q5. SCAL1 starts the routine Relative::CountBlocks and SSCALC is its Start entry, which callers use; it falls
  into Relative::AddToCount.
- D-P5-Q6. DIV254 and DIV120 stay separate routines (DivideBy254, DivideBy120) with the BIT-skipped load explained and
  an assert that DivideBy120 follows.
- D-P5-Q7. R35 and R40, both at $E253, are one label Relative::PositionCommand::SetOffset with both names in Also.
- D-P5-Q8. RECORD, the P command, is Relative::PositionCommand, reached only through the command table.
- D-P5-Q9. NXOUT, RD15, RD40, RD05, ADDR1 and RDS5 are entries; GETHDR, ACCX2, ACC200, ADDT12, BHERE2 and DIV120 are
  routines fallen into; BREAK is Relative::SideSectorError; NXTR35, WR10, AR55 and NS50 are locals.
- D-P5-Q10. Where the listing's comments are wrong (POSBUF, NXTREC, BREAK's literal, NULBUF's "cr" that stores $FF) the
  reasons and new comments describe the code.
- D-P5-Q11. B0TOB0 is kept byte for byte; counts 1 to 129 copy that many bytes and 0 or 130 to 255 copy one, documented
  in the routine's comment.

### Part 6: the serial bus, the idle loop, start-up, the errors and the ROM's tables and vectors

- D-P6-Q1. The serial bus takes the kernal's names, from the device's end, with ILERR, FRMERR and ITERR as
  `Serial::BusError`.
- D-P6-Q2. ACP01/ACP03 and FRMERR/ITERR/ILERR share one name each (`Serial::BusError` with entries), and MAXTRK/TRKNUM
  stay two labels with an `.assert`.
- D-P6-Q3. The error table is `Errors::Messages` written with a `Message` macro and `Errors::WORD_*` token constants,
  with `Errors::MessagesEnd` documenting the one-byte overrun, and ETEND sharing ERROR's address.
- D-P6-Q4. The ROM's tables are top-level, named for what they hold, `CommandPatterns` is a new label at `$FEAD` with
  STRUCT an equate `CommandPatterns - 8` named `PATTERN_ORIGIN`, and the `Bit*` bytes stay ROM bytes.
- D-P6-Q5. UBLOCK is `UserCommandVectors` (pointed at by `Commands::UserVectorsPointer`), the two words before it are
  `FormatVector` and `MotorOffVector`, NNMI is `Startup::Nmi` and NMI is `Startup::Reinitialize`.
- D-P6-Q6. The patches are routines of their own in their families, named with `Patch`, and the labels they return to
  are entries.
- D-P6-Q7. Each revision difference is one `.if` on the revision's constant at its place, with the corrected addresses
  (`$E683`, `$E68B`, `$E9DC`, `$EE3D`) and four changes for the 1540; -06's patch, which has no listing name, is
  `Commands::FormatPatch`, built for R6 only.
- D-P6-Q8. Unreached labels are dropped, their code kept with a comment, and MAPOFF stays as data.
- D-P6-Q9. SETLDS is top-level `TurnOnActivityLed` (three families call it), ERRON and ERROFF are
  `Errors::StartFlashing` and `Errors::StopFlashing`, and KILLP is `Commands::DisableProtection`.
- D-P6-Q10. The code's slips (including TLERR freeing a stale channel on a failed FNDRCH) are documented, not changed.

## Step 4: the DOS

`Serial/Dos1541.asm` and the ten files it includes: the equates, then nine in address order from `$C000`, each named
for what most of it is (`Dos1541.Commands.asm` to `Dos1541.Tables.asm`). A script wrote the first version from the
dumps, outside the repository: each instruction from the ROM's bytes at the address the listing's instruction
assembles to, data where the listing has data, the scopes, routines, entries and locals the rename table gives, each
operand by name where the listing's operand names that value, and each definition's `Also:` and first comment from
the table. Each file's routines are then documented by hand, with their contracts, as the kernal's were.

The builds are chosen by `REVISION`: 1, 2, 3, 5 or 6 for 901229-01 to -06, and 40 for the 1540's 325303-01. Three
flags it gives name what differs (D-P2-Q7, D-P6-Q7): `DRIVE_1540` (the 1540's message 73, its bit loop without the
speed test, no SlowDown, CLKDAT or NNMI, the NMI vector at Reinitialize, and the `NOP` before `FillData`);
`LATE_PATCHES`, -05 and -06 (the patches PATCH5 and PATCH6, the boot loader removed, the bus no longer released at the
error channel, VIA 1's port B set before its directions); and `SINGLE_BIT_READ`, from -02 (a bit taken with CLK and
DATA in one read). The header gap (`HEADER_GAP`) is 10 in R1, R2 and R40 and 11 from R3, `RSR` is in R2's and R3's
fill, -06's `FormatPatch` is built in R6 alone, and each build has its own checksum byte.

Every build is byte-identical to its chips (R1, R2, R3, R5, R6 and R40), and `Dos1541SourceTests` pins each by digest;
`BuildLinkTests.Dos1541LinksOnlyWhatItsBuildAssembles` checks each build's links, and the editor's lint is clean. The
drives run from the builds: `Cbm1541SdkModules` assembles a drive's ROMs, the VIC-20's debug target uses them when
MAME's `c1541` or `c1540` set is not found (901229-06 for a 1541, as MAME's default), and
`Dos1541SourceTests.DriveRunsFromTheSdkBuilds` has a VIC-20, itself from the SDK's builds, format a disk, save a
program and load it back through each build.

What the conversion changed in the table (its rows now give the source's names):

- The boot loader's three labels, dropped for -05 (D-P3-Q8), name code in R1, R2, R3 and R40, which keep the loader:
  BOOT2 is `Startup::BootExit`, BOOT3 `Startup::Boot::WaitForClip` and BOOT4 `Commands::LoadUtility::AfterName`.
- New names, where the listing has no label (the table's questions give them): `Controller::VerifyTrack` (`$FD27`),
  `CommandPatterns` (`$FEAD`, with STRUCT, `PATTERN_ORIGIN`, eight bytes before it), `FormatVector` and
  `MotorOffVector` (`$FFE6`, `$FFE8`), -06's `Commands::FormatPatch`, and `SerialNoDelay`, drive 1's byte of
  `Controller::HeadTrack`, which the listing writes as DRVTRK+1.
- A routine named as a member of another (`Commands::LoadUtility::GetByte`, the B- command's tables) is nested in it,
  since its code follows its parent's.
